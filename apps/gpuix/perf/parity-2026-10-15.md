### Confirmed regression and failing checks

`combined-pointer-check.ts` makes the same Settings switch click and Space press, checking the scratch daemon after each. Repeated result:

```text
Current native: pointer changed worktrees_default: false
Current native: Space changed worktrees_default: true
AssertionError: Pointer click did not reach the switch; compare islands-prev.node
Previous islands binary: pointer changed worktrees_default: true
Previous islands binary: Space changed worktrees_default: true
```

Both appearances of `settings-check.ts` fail `Switch did not round-trip through settings.update`. Keyboard writes work. This is evidence of a full-shell native interaction regression, despite the six isolated native labs passing. Its precise native cause is not isolated; no speculative production workaround was added. The full-app tour also logs `Warning: Window move completed without beginning` after title-bar interaction. Investigate cached-island hitboxes, nested drag regions, and pointer event routing together.

`dock-check.ts` and the corrected `dock2-check.ts` fail `Timed out waiting for dock entrance complete` with ordinary real-time background animation. The controlled-clock whole-app tour can open the dock with Cmd-J and visit every tab. Do not claim foreground drag/motion parity from this workaround; isolate occlusion/animation and hitbox behavior with the native regression above.
