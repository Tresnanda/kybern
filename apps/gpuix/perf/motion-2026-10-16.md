# Task and Environment motion — October 16, 2026

## Integration decision

Only the TypeScript transition lifecycle, task opacity/timing and corrected
shadow fixes are integrated. Native rotation, its package/type patch, and the
measured addon remain in the motion5 worktree and archived `motion5.patch`;
they are **not accepted or enabled** because the carry resource gate is unresolved.
The native measurements below describe that candidate, not the integrated addon.
Board rotation and full resource/motion parity remain unchecked.

Parent integration checks on the accepted addon passed Environment curves, reopen
interruption and immediate keyboard/reduced behavior in dark/light modes. Task
board/list settle checks passed dark/light normal and reduced; the first reduced-list
synthetic pickup timed out, then an isolated repeat passed. This remains an
automation reliability limitation, not a claim of physical pointer certification.
The native candidate measurements below must not be attributed to this subset.


The source curves, durations, return motion and reduced-motion paths are verified.
The final strengthened carry resource gate is **blocked**: the accepted addon
canceled the synthetic held ghost before the 15 s interval ended, so a warmed
comparison cannot be accepted this round.
Whole-app 1:1 motion parity is **partial**: the Environment transition still
submits native scenes around 35–38ms median and 51–53ms p95. The desktop
frontend's headless WebKit reference is around 17 ms median/18–22ms p95.
Scene submission and browser rAF are different stages; neither is a physical
display-presentation or OS input-latency measurement. No 60 fps claim is made.

## Full-app measurements

Apple M1, macOS 27, Bun 1.4.0, GPUIX 0.10.0. One visible but nonactivating native
window, 10 s warmup; 15 s measured scene intervals. No screenshots during resource
sampling. CPU is cumulative process CPU time, GPU is per-PID AGX accumulated
time, RAM is physical footprint. Daemon and WindowServer are excluded. Host
activity was not controlled as a dedicated benchmark machine.

Pre-crop native layer samples (the layer is absent in these three scenes):

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settled eight-turn thread |1.86% /1.6 /174 |1.93% /3.9 /192 |1.60% /3.4 /191 |
| Repeated Environment open/close |18.66% /358.5 /187 |20.57% /334.8 /205 |21.55% /329.3 /203 |
| Board, animated empty Running header |2.06% /60.8 /207 |2.73% /72.1 /207 |2.73% /71.6 /206 |

Each idle interval has only 2–3 scene submissions and 3 React commits, not a
continuous native redraw. The rejected experiment's 40% idle/1424ms GPU result
was not reproduced or imported. These comparisons are between rebuilt and
accepted addon binaries, so the 18 MiB thread difference is reported rather than
attributed entirely to rotation. They do not establish a saving.

Environment scene-submission p95: accepted 51.1 ms, new 52.0/52.7 ms. Automation RPC
to first observed changed native bounds p95: accepted 42.5 ms, new 60.6/52.5 ms.
These probes include transport and can miss the first changed frame; they are
not OS input-to-screen latency. React commit counts are recorded, but commit
duration is not available from this measurement hook. No energy measurement.

The first window-sized rotation prototype used 23.0% CPU,1317ms GPU and231MiB
during continuous board carry (15s), returning to 214 MiB after release. It was
replaced by the cropped layer; it is **not** the final implementation.
Initial cropped-layer pair before CSS-to-sigma shadow correction (same 15 s continuous board carry):

| Addon | CPU | GPU | Footprint | Submission interval median / p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted | 23.33% | 985.7 ms | 207 MiB | 17.39 / 35.74 ms |
| Cropped rotation | 27.79% | 1053.2 ms | 209 MiB | 17.29 / 40.76 ms |

After release: 207 / 214 MiB respectively and zero held ghosts. The observed
start-to-native-bounds probes are 25.4 / 29.2 ms, including automation transport.
The 4.46 percentage-point CPU increase exceeds the earlier 3-point noise gate;
this result **does not pass the carry resource gate**. The sampler now has an
explicit 1 s held-layer warmup and asserts the ghost remains held at the end.
The accepted addon failed that guard with both stationary and moving warmup,
even with the fixture isolated from physical mouse events. Those attempts are
invalid resource samples, and the new addon was not compared against an invalid
baseline. Their logs are `accepted-warm.log` and `accepted-warm-moving.log`.
The exact source of the synthetic cancellation is unresolved; no production
release/cancellation guard was removed to make the test pass. Earlier carry
samples lacked that final hold assertion, so they are provisional and cannot
establish resource acceptance. The observed 4.46-point increase is retained,
not explained away. Cropping reduced the prototype's texture cost, but does not
establish a saving over the accepted addon. No further UI iteration this round.
