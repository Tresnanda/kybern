# Mobile motion verification — 2026-09-09

The composer transition follows the spatial continuity in the supplied reference:
https://x.com/kedia_naman/status/2097362796644118640

Sheets now share their entrance and drag offset, preserve release velocity, and
use a following outline for stretch and recoil. Only the clipping silhouette
changes shape; page content keeps its final dimensions and readable opaque
Android surface. Dialogs without a measured source fade at their final bounds.
The composer attachment menu originates from its measured plus button.

Sending captures the actual text and attachment positions before the request.
A successful RPC receipt identifies the destination row; image asset identities
survive daemon normalization to inline images. One UI-thread geometry read tracks
the destination while the keyboard moves. At most one flight exists, with a
bounded timeout and cancellation when the app backgrounds. Failed sends preserve
the draft. Queued messages and structured multi-text inputs use normal rendering.
Reduced motion bypasses send flights and fades sheets.

## Verification

- Android 15 / API 35 arm64 Pixel 7 emulator, release Hermes APK, on an Apple M1
  Mac with 16 GB RAM. Native release networking exercised a local fixture server.
- Recorded and inspected text and image sends with keyboard dismissal. The image
  fixture normalizes uploaded attachments just as the daemon does. Verified the
  final image and exact text remain visible after the flight.
- Deliberately rejected a send and verified the error and original text remain.
- Inspected light composer and dark sheet surfaces, source-anchored attachment
  menu, sheet expansion, short drag return, fast dismissal, and reduced-motion
  sheet open/close after restarting with Android animator duration scale zero.
- Mobile typecheck, 49 tests, Expo Doctor (21/21), and Android/iOS export passed.
- Desktop typecheck, lint, build, 111 transcript tests, and native WebKit artifact
  integration fixtures in light and dark passed.
- Relevant Rust daemon and desktop library tests, formatting, and clippy passed.
  Scratch-daemon checks retained both the port and redeemed pairing across a
  restart, rejected an occupied saved port, and removed endpoint files on SIGINT.

Local clips live under ignored `artifacts/mobile-motion/`: `release-send.mp4`,
`release-image-send.mp4`, and `release-sheet.mp4`. Earlier Expo Go clips and the
older installed APK are exploratory evidence, not an exact pre-change baseline.
The release build used local NDK 27.1 via a temporary Gradle init script and had
OTA updates disabled in its generated native configuration; no native config
change is included in the source patch.

These are functional and visual checks, not frame-rate or energy measurements.
Physical-device motion feel and native iOS execution remain unverified. No FPS,
CPU, commit-time, input-latency, or battery improvement is claimed. New-thread
receipt matching is unit-tested but was not exercised by the native fixture.
